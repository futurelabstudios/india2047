**Theme:** Education

## The problem today

Many children in India learn in a language that isn't the one they speak at home, and access to good one-on-one help depends heavily on where a family lives and what it can afford.

## India in 2047

Meera, 11, lives in a village in Odisha. After school she asks questions out loud in Odia to a free AI tutor on a shared community tablet. It explains fractions using examples from her mother's market stall, then switches to English when she wants to practise. Her teacher sees a weekly summary of where each student is stuck and spends class time on those topics. Every school and library has these tutors, and they work offline.

## How we got there

- Open, high-quality speech and text models for all 22 scheduled languages, trained with community-contributed data.
- Curriculum-aligned lesson content released under open licences.
- Low-cost, offline-capable devices placed in schools, libraries and panchayat offices.
- Teacher training focused on using tutor insights, not replacing teachers.
- Strong privacy rules for children's data.

## Start this year

Record and openly license 100 everyday maths and science explanations in your own language, and share them in this repository.
