# Code of Conduct

India2047 is a space to imagine a better future together. We want it to be welcoming to everyone, regardless of age, caste, religion, region, language, gender, disability, background or experience.

## Our standards

**Do**

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Critique ideas, not people.
- Build on others' ideas and credit them.
- Welcome newcomers and people writing in any Indian language.

**Don't**

- Harass, insult or demean anyone.
- Post hateful, communal, sexual or violent content.
- Share others' private information.
- Spam, self-promote or plagiarise.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove content and may block contributors who break these standards. Report concerns by opening a confidential report to the maintainers via GitHub (Report content) or by contacting the Futurelab Studios maintainers through the organisation profile.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
