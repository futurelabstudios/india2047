# Code of Conduct

India 2047 is a celebration of a shared future, and this community should feel like one.

## Our pledge

We welcome everyone regardless of age, body size, caste, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, region, language, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, patient and constructive.
- Assume good intent; give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Respect every region, culture and faith represented in the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Hateful, communal or discriminatory content in code, comments, issues or PRs
- Publishing others' private information
- Political campaigning or partisan content

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and block contributors, that break this code. Report issues to the maintainers at shabbir@futurelabstudios.com. Reports are handled confidentially.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
